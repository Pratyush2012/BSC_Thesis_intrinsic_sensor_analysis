\section*{Acknowledgements}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Acknowledgements}

I would like to thank my supervisor, Jens Christian Andersen, for his patient guidance throughout this project. While the original idea of using a robot's intrinsic motion sensors for terrain classification was mine, it was Christian who suggested framing the work around robotic intercropping, which gave the thesis a concrete and meaningful application context. He also pointed me towards Blanke et al.'s \emph{Diagnosis and Fault-Tolerant Control} (1st ed., 2003), whose change-detection chapter became the foundation of the CUSUM-based transition analysis in \Cref{ch:results}, and proposed a 1D convolutional network as a deep-learning alternative to the feature-based classifiers, which I subsequently implemented and evaluated alongside the classical baselines.

I am also grateful to the team at \emph{DTU Electro} for developing \emph{Ricbot}, the robotic platform used for every data-collection run in this thesis. Without their hardware, firmware and prior software work, the project simply would not have been possible.

Finally, I would like to thank my family and friends for their continued support and encouragement throughout the eighteen weeks of this project.
